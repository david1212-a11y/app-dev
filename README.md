## Movies

### Men in Black
**Men in Black** is a *sci-fi comedy* about a secret agency that monitors aliens living on Earth.

- Main characters: **Agent J** and **Agent K**
- Famous gadget: the **neuralyzer** that erases memories
- Why I like it:
  - Funny and action-packed
  - Creative alien designs
  - Great chemistry between the two leads

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_(1997_film))

### The Matrix
**The Matrix** asks a big question: *what if the world around us isn't real?*

> "Welcome to the real world." – Morpheus

1. Neo discovers the truth about the simulation
2. He chooses the **red pill**
3. He joins the rebels and learns to bend the rules of the Matrix

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Matrix)

## Series

### Game of Thrones
An epic fantasy about noble houses fighting for the **Iron Throne**. It is based on the book series *A Song of Ice and Fire*.[^1]

- Great houses: **Stark**, **Lannister**, **Targaryen**
- Themes: power, loyalty, betrayal, and survival

> Winter is coming.

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones)

### That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (Tensura)
An anime about a regular office worker who is reincarnated as a **slime** in a fantasy world and slowly builds his own nation.

- Main character: **Rimuru Tempest**
- Genre: isekai, fantasy, action
- What I enjoy: the nation-building and how the world grows with every season

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/That_Time_I_Got_Reincarnated_as_a_Slime)

### The Walking Dead
A survival drama set in a world overrun by zombies, where the *living* are often more dangerous than the dead.

- Lead character: **Rick Grimes**
- Focus: survival, tough choices, and rebuilding society

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series))

### Breaking Bad
A high school chemistry teacher turns to a life of crime after a life-changing diagnosis. Watching **Walter White** transform over five seasons is unforgettable.

- Created by **Vince Gilligan**
- Main duo: **Walter White** and **Jesse Pinkman**

[Read more on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad)
